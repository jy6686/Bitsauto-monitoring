One additional governance rule I'd adopt

Since you've established four governance documents, I'd add one cross-cutting engineering rule that applies to every future sprint:

Single Source of Truth Rule

Every business metric, KPI, report, AI model, reconciliation, invoice, settlement, and dashboard must identify its canonical source in the Finance Data Dictionary. If a module derives or stores the same metric independently, the duplication must be explicitly documented and justified.

This rule complements the governance you've already established by preventing KPI drift over time.
