I agree with that direction. Given the state you've reached, I would avoid calling it "Sprint D" until its scope is clearly defined. Instead, I'd frame it as the next product milestone:

Commercial Workspace v2 — Execution Layer

The guiding objective becomes:

Reduce the time from identifying an issue to completing the business action.

That naturally follows the progression you've established:

Operational
      ↓
Intelligence
      ↓
Action
      ↓
Execution
Execution Principles

To preserve the architecture you've frozen, I'd adopt three permanent rules:

Rule 1 — Actions originate from Intelligence

No standalone workflow pages.

Every execution starts from an Action Center item or an existing workspace section.

Examples:

Pending Rate Push → Open approval
Balance Warning → Open account
Quality Alert → Open Live Traffic
Revenue Drop → Open Client
Rule 2 — Execute in Context

Avoid taking the user to another application.

Instead:

Action Item
      ↓
Slide-over / Drawer
      ↓
Execute
      ↓
Refresh Workspace

The Commercial Workspace remains the user's working environment.

Rule 3 — Every Action Produces an Audit Event

Since you already have governance and a Trust Center direction, every execution should emit an auditable event.

For example:

Action
      ↓
Validation
      ↓
Execution
      ↓
Audit Event
      ↓
Workspace Refresh

This makes execution traceable without changing the workspace architecture.

What I'd avoid

I would deliberately avoid adding new top-level navigation such as:

Workflow
Tasks
Approvals
CRM
Audit

Those concepts already have natural homes:

Action Center → inbox
Clients → customer context
Products → rate operations
Reports → outcomes
Trust Center (if applicable) → governance and audit visibility

Keeping execution embedded within the existing workspace preserves the simplicity you've achieved.

Overall Assessment

At this stage, the Commercial Workspace has a clear product trajectory:

v1 established a hierarchy-aware operational workspace.
v2 can focus on completing the business execution loop—turning insights into actions, actions into outcomes, and outcomes into an auditable history—without altering the underlying architecture or expanding the navigation model.
