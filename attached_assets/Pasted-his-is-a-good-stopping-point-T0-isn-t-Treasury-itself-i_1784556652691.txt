Recommendation before T1

Before writing Treasury dashboard code, define a Treasury Data Contract similar to what you did for Finance. It should answer, for every KPI:

KPI	Source	Calculation	Owner
Outstanding Receivables	Invoice Batches + Payments	Invoice Balance − Payments	Treasury
Due Today	Settlements	Due Date = Invoice Date + Due Days	Treasury
Overdue	Settlements	Due Date < Today	Treasury
Cash Position	Cash Ledger	Ledger Balance	Treasury
Forecast	Forecast Engine	Based on Settlements	Treasury

This ensures every Treasury metric has a single source of truth from day one and preserves the governance discipline that made the Finance implementation successful.
