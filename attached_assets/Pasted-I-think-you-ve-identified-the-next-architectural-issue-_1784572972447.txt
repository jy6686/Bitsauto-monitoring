I think you've identified the next architectural issue correctly. Right now the Commercial Portal is acting as a launcher into the Main Platform, instead of being a self-contained workspace.

From your screenshots, I can see:

Commercial Portal Home has workflow cards (Rate Manager, KAM Dashboard, etc.).
Clicking many of those cards navigates to existing main-platform pages like /clients, /products, etc.
The KAM Dashboard quick actions (e.g. Send Rate) also navigate to the legacy Clients page (/clients?tab=send-rate), not a Commercial Portal page.
Your target Commercial Portal is intentionally much smaller than the current inventory.
I agree with reducing the scope

The Commercial Portal should only expose these modules:

Top Menu	Access
Dashboard	Read-only (hierarchy filtered)
Live Calls	Read-only (hierarchy filtered)
Live Traffic	Read-only (hierarchy filtered)
BitsEye	Read-only (hierarchy filtered)
Clients	Read-only (hierarchy filtered)
Balance	Read-only (hierarchy filtered)
Products (Rate Manager)	Read/Write (according to permissions)
Commercial Reports	Read-only (hierarchy filtered)

Everything else can remain implemented in the platform but should not appear in the Commercial Portal.

Features to remove from the Commercial Portal

I agree with removing these from the Commercial navigation and home page:

❌ Destination Catalog
❌ Revenue Heatmap
❌ Carrier Intelligence
❌ Executive Reports
❌ Cost Optimisation
❌ Dispute Defense
❌ Product Registry

Those can remain in the codebase and even the main platform if they have other consumers, but they should not be surfaced to Commercial users through this portal.

The bigger problem: navigation

The issue is not just the home page cards.

Today the flow is effectively:

Commercial Portal
        ↓
Click Clients
        ↓
Main Platform (/clients)

It should become:

Commercial Portal
        ↓
Click Clients
        ↓
Commercial Clients

The same applies to:

Dashboard
Live Calls
Live Traffic
BitsEye
Balance
Products
Commercial Reports

Each top menu should stay inside the Commercial Portal shell.

Recommended implementation order

I would treat this as a dedicated sprint with four steps:

Trim the Commercial Portal
Remove the workflow cards and menu items you listed.
Keep only the eight approved top menus.
Portal-local routing
Change navigation so menu clicks stay under /commercial/... rather than jumping to /clients, /products, etc.
Hierarchy integration
Wire each Commercial page to the Hierarchy Scope Service.
Apply the data filter before rendering.
Retire legacy links
Update KAM Dashboard quick actions (like Send Rate) to point to the Commercial Products/Rate Manager page instead of /clients?tab=send-rate.

This gives you a true portal experience where users remain in the Commercial workspace instead of being dropped back into the main platform.

I would make portal-local routing the next priority, because until the navigation stays within /commercial, the Commercial Portal will continue to feel like a collection of shortcuts rather than its own application.

The biggest architectural improvement

Don't create:

NOCDashboard.tsx

CommercialDashboard.tsx

Create one reusable dashboard.

Dashboard Component
        │
        ├── Data Provider (NOC)
        │
        └── Data Provider (Commercial)

The cards, charts, tables, and layout stay identical.

Only the data provider changes.

That matches the architecture you've been building:

NOC Portal = global operational visibility.
Commercial Portal = hierarchy-filtered operational visibility.

This gives users a consistent experience while ensuring each portal sees only the data appropriate to its role.
