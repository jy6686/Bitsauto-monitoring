What I'd verify

First, check whether AMI is actually exposed to the Internet:

ss -ltnp | grep 5038

If you see something like:

0.0.0.0:5038

then AMI is listening on all interfaces.

If you see:

127.0.0.1:5038

or your private management IP only, that's much better.

Next, check your firewall:

firewall-cmd --list-ports
firewall-cmd --list-services

or, if you're using iptables:

iptables -L -n

Finally, inspect the AMI configuration:

grep -A10 "\[bitsauto\]" /etc/asterisk/manager.conf

I'm looking for entries like:

permit=
deny=

or a bind address such as:

bindaddr=
