Frozen Governance Baseline

Status: ✅ Version 1.0 – Frozen

Canonical Rules

Hierarchy Scope (Mandatory)
Every widget, KPI, chart, table, alert, and report must derive its data from the authenticated user's hierarchy scope.
No Global Queries
Global operational queries are prohibited in the Commercial Dashboard.
Portfolio KPIs Only
Every KPI must be derived from the authenticated user's visible account set.
Unsupported Data Models
Metrics that cannot be hierarchy-filtered must not appear until the underlying data model supports portfolio scoping.

Design Principle

The Commercial Dashboard is a portfolio management dashboard, not a network operations dashboard. It answers "How are my customers performing?" rather than "How is the platform performing?"
