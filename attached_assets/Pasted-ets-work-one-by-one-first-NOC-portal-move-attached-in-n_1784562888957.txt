The key is to define what "Email Centre" means.

If it's:

Customer outage notifications
Maintenance notifications
Low balance alerts
Route migration notices
Technical announcements
Vendor maintenance notifications
Network maintenance emails

then it is an operational communication tool, and it belongs in the NOC Portal.

Similarly, if NOC is responsible for creating and configuring customers and vendors technically, then the NOC Portal should own all technical client/vendor configuration.

Ownership rule

I think we've arrived at a clearer governance rule:

NOC Portal owns technical operations and technical client/vendor configuration.
Commercial Portal owns business relationships and commercial activities.
Finance Portal owns financial operations.
Admin/Main Platform owns platform administration.

That means we won't decide based on the page title (for example, "Clients" or "Email Centre"). We'll decide based on business ownership.

If NOC is responsible for configuring it, operating it, or communicating technical events about it, it belongs in the NOC Portal—even if the feature is under a "Clients" menu today.
