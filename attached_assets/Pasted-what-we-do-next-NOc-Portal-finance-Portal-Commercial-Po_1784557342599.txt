My recommendation

I would not start Treasury immediately.

Instead, I would use the certified Finance backend to complete the Platform Foundation and Portal Framework. Once authentication, portal assignment, RBAC, and routing are stable, you can expose the already-built Finance capabilities through the Finance Portal while simultaneously making the NOC Portal production-ready.

That sequence minimizes rework because every subsequent portal will inherit the same authentication, authorization, navigation, and configuration framework rather than implementing those concerns independently.

In that case, don't build anything new. Migrate and certify.

From our earlier discussions, your portal framework already exists. The remaining work is to move the existing features into their correct portal, remove duplicates, and verify permissions.

I would approach it in this order:

Phase	Portal	What to do	Status Goal
1	NOC Portal	Move all operational features (Live Calls, Live Traffic, Traffic Map, OPS Console, Server Health, Alerts, SIP tools, etc.)	First production portal
2	Finance Portal	Move all Finance features (Dashboard, Snapshot, Reconciliation, Billing, Treasury, Reports, Audit)	Finance v1.0 portal
3	Commercial Portal	Move CRM, Clients, Vendors, Contracts, Rate Cards, Routing Requests, Margin Analysis, Account Managers	Commercial operations
4	Product Portal	Move Destination Catalog, Products, Rate Plans, Prefix Management, Routing Products, Pricing Templates	Product governance
5	Partner / Client Portals	Expose only the approved self-service features	External users
Migration rules

For every feature you move:

✅ One module = one owner portal.
✅ No duplicate pages across portals.
✅ Shared backend/API remains the same.
✅ Shared React component remains the same where possible.
✅ Only routing, navigation, and permissions change.

For example:

Live Calls

Before:

/live-calls

After:

/noc/live-calls

The page itself shouldn't be rewritten—only its location and access control change.

Likewise:

Financial Snapshot

Moves from the generic application to:

/finance/financial-snapshot

without changing the underlying service.

Recommended migration sequence

I would migrate by business impact, not alphabetically:

NOC Portal – highest operational value.
Finance Portal – backend already certified.
Commercial Portal – daily business users.
Product Portal – governance and catalog.
Remaining portals.
Definition of Done

A portal is complete only when:

All assigned features are accessible from that portal.
No feature appears in the wrong portal.
RBAC works correctly.
URLs use the portal prefix.
Cross-portal navigation is removed unless explicitly intended.
The original application menu no longer exposes those migrated features.

At this point, I wouldn't allocate time to new modules. I'd focus on portal migration, certification, and cleanup until each portal is complete. Once all portals are certified, you'll have a clean, role-based platform instead of a single application with mixed responsibilities.
