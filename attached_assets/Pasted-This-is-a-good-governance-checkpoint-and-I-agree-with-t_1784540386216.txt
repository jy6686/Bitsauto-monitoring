One recommendation before F3

Before starting F3 (Reconciliation & AI Evidence), I would add one more governance artifact—not because it's missing today, but because it will become important as more Finance modules depend on the snapshot.

Finance Data Dictionary

Create a document such as:

.local/governance/finance-data-dictionary.md

It should define every canonical financial metric once, for example:

Metric	Definition	Source
Revenue	Sum of rated sell amounts	financial_snapshot.sell_amount
Cost	Sum of rated buy amounts	financial_snapshot.buy_amount
Margin	Revenue − Cost	financial_snapshot.margin_amount
Margin %	Margin ÷ Revenue × 100	Derived
Billable Minutes	Sum of billed seconds ÷ 60	Snapshot
Active Clients	Distinct client accounts in snapshot	Snapshot
Active Vendors	Distinct vendors in snapshot	Snapshot
Revenue Leakage	Difference between expected and billed revenue	Defined rule

The purpose is to ensure:

Finance Cockpit
Revenue Drift
AI Assurance
Reconciliation
Billing
Treasury

all use the same definition for every KPI.

This avoids subtle inconsistencies where different modules calculate "Margin" or "Revenue" differently over time.
