These three tickets are well-scoped and follow the right dependency chain. I would make a few adjustments before implementation to avoid future refactoring.

Overall sequence

I would execute them in this order:

F1 → F3 → F2

instead of

F1 → F2 → F3

Reason:

F1 establishes the canonical financial dataset.
F3 validates that dataset through reconciliation and AI evidence.
F2 (invoice generation) should consume the validated financial snapshot rather than generating invoices from potentially inconsistent upstream data.

Invoice generation is a financial "publisher." It should be one of the last consumers of a validated dataset, not one of the first.
