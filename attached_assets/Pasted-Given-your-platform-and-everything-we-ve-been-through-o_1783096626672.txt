Immediate (today)
1. Apply logrotate ✅

Yes. I recommend replacing the "truncate every 6 hours" approach with proper log rotation for everything except recordings.

For recordings, keep your current cleanup policy because those are large media files.

For logs, use rotation instead of destroying them.

Example:

cat >/etc/logrotate.d/asterisk-all <<'EOF'
/var/log/asterisk/*.log
{
    daily
    rotate 14
    compress
    delaycompress
    missingok
    notifempty
    copytruncate
    create 0640 asterisk asterisk
}
EOF

I would remove this cron afterwards:

0 */6 * * * > /var/log/asterisk/fail2ban && > /var/log/asterisk/freepbx.log

because logrotate should manage logs.

Keep the recording cleanup cron.

Offer safe actions such as:

Notice the emphasis on Preview cleanup first. Given your production-first preference, avoid automatic deletion without showing what will happen.

I would NOT auto-restart services

One thing I would not implement is:

systemctl restart asterisk
systemctl restart mariadb

automatically.

There are situations where an automatic restart can make diagnosis harder or interrupt active traffic.

Instead:

Detect the issue.
Notify immediately.
Provide a one-click/manual action if appropriate.
