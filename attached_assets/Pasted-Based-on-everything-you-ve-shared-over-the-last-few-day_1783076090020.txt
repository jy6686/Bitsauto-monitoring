The statement:

"Sippy is not routing OTP traffic through the governance Asterisk."

This is a strong architectural conclusion. Before accepting it, I would capture one live OTP call end-to-end and verify:

Sippy selected gateway 159.223.32.59 (or did not).
An INVITE reached that Asterisk (or did not).
core show channels reflected the call (or did not).
BitsAuto's AMI listener saw (or did not see) the corresponding Bridge events.

If those four checks all indicate that the governance Asterisk never sees the call, then the root cause is confirmed. Until then, I would describe it as the leading hypothesis, not yet a proven resolution.

That distinction is important because it prevents another round of production changes based on an assumption rather than verified evidence.
