I agree with your sequencing: A → B is the strongest path because it maximizes business value while preserving momentum.

Sprint A — Commercial Operations Completion

This sprint should have one objective:

Eliminate the remaining "bridge" implementations so every Commercial section follows the canonical backend pattern.

Deliverables
1. Balance (highest priority)

Replace:

/api/sippy/balance-monitor
        ↓
Client-side filtering

with:

resolveCommercialScope()
        ↓
/api/commercial/balance
        ↓
Portfolio balances

This makes Balance consistent with Clients, Live Calls, and Live Traffic.

2. Reports

Replace static links with Commercial-native report views:

Revenue
Forecast
Profit / Loss

All portfolio-scoped and read-only.

Even if V1 simply wraps existing report data, the user should remain inside the Commercial Workspace.

3. Products

Keep the current Rate Manager integration, but remove the feeling of a "link-out."

The Commercial workspace should present:

Products
 ├── Rate Analysis
 ├── Send Rate
 └── Push History

using the existing architecture and hierarchy scope.

Sprint B — Commercial Intelligence

This is where the product becomes genuinely differentiated.

I wouldn't think of it as "Live Traffic improvements."

I'd define it as:

Portfolio Intelligence

Instead of only showing live call counts, expose operational insight for a KAM's portfolio.

Intelligence panels

Traffic

Top customers by volume
Active destinations
Active vendors
Hourly trend

Quality

ASR
ACD
MOS
PDD
Jitter (where available)

Risk

Traffic drops
Quality degradation
Zero-traffic customers
Balance warnings
Route instability

Commercial

Revenue trend
Margin trend (if available)
Top growth accounts
Accounts requiring attention

The goal is that a KAM can open the workspace each morning and immediately know which customers need attention.

Suggested roadmap

At this point I'd formalize the roadmap like this:

Sprint	Theme	Status
Foundation	Workspace architecture	✅ Complete
A	Operations Completion	Next
B	Portfolio Intelligence	Planned
C	Action Center	Future

That keeps each sprint focused:

Foundation established the architecture.
Sprint A removes the remaining inconsistencies.
Sprint B delivers the features that distinguish the Commercial Portal.
Sprint C introduces workflow and task management only after the analytical foundation is mature.

This sequence builds on the frozen architecture without introducing unnecessary complexity, and it keeps each sprint aligned with a clear business objective.
