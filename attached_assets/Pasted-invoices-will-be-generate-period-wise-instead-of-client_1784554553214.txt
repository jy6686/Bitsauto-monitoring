I actually agree with changing the workflow. For a wholesale telecom carrier platform, the invoice should be generated by billing period first, not by manually selecting a client.

The current dialog is ERP-like:

Select Client → Select Period → Generate Invoice

That doesn't scale for telecom billing.

This also fits perfectly with the architecture you've already established:

F1 provides the Financial Snapshot.
F3 certifies and reconciles the snapshot.
F2 generates invoices from a validated billing period, not from an individual client.

That is much closer to how wholesale VoIP carriers typically operate, where finance closes an entire billing cycle and then generates invoices for all eligible accounts in that cycle, with the ability to regenerate or manually invoice individual clients only as an exception.
