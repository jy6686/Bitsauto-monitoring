Governance recommendation

I would add one permanent rule to the certification template:

No sprint may be certified solely on successful compilation, startup, or code inspection. Certification requires evidence that the implemented capability behaves correctly under representative operational conditions, with validation directly traceable to the sprint's acceptance criteria.
