One governance rule I'd add to the document

The principle shouldn't just say:

"The menu name can be the same across portals."

I'd add:

Portal membership is determined at the feature level, not the menu level. A menu is only a grouping mechanism. Features are independently assigned to one or more portals based on business ownership and operational need.

That captures exactly what you've been describing:

NOC owns operational features.
Finance owns financial features.
Commercial owns customer/business features.
Admin owns platform administration.

A menu title like Clients is just a container; it does not imply the same contents in every portal.
