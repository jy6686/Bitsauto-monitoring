Future-proofing

One enhancement I'd recommend is supporting historical payment terms via effective_from/effective_to, as you suggested. This ensures invoices continue to use the terms that were in force when they were issued, even if a client's terms change later. Treasury should copy the resolved due_days (and resulting due_date) into the settlement record at creation time, rather than recalculating against current settings.

That preserves the same immutability principle you've already established with:

snapshot_run_id
recon_run_id

The settlement record becomes a snapshot of the commercial agreement at the time the obligation was created.
