Why it matters

This is acceptable during development, but in production you generally want historical snapshots to remain available so reconciliation, AI findings, and invoices continue to reference the exact dataset they were built from.

If your current materialization process physically replaces rows, it's worth confirming that this is intentional. If the long-term goal is immutable history (which your governance suggests), then retaining prior snapshot runs is preferable.
