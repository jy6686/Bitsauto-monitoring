What we also decided about configuration

One important governance decision was that menus are not hard-coded.

The navigation is driven by the database (Portal Assignment Manager), so modules can be:

moved between portals,
assigned to multiple portals,
hidden,
exposed as read-only,
reordered,
added later,

without changing application code. That flexibility was a requirement you emphasized from the beginning.
