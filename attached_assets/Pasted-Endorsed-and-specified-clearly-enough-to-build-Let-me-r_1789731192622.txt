The production upload already does exactly what you asked for. It builds a one-row workbook with buildRateXlsx(action, null, prefix, ...), where the second argument is the rate id and it is passed as null. The action is A for a future date and SA for immediate. That is precisely Sippy's documented creation contract: Action A with the id left blank so Sippy allocates one.

It does not download and patch anything. The builder I examined yesterday, which drops absent prefixes, is called from exactly one place: the debug endpoint that generates a workbook for inspection. Its own comment says it mirrors the push path. I treated a mirror as the thing itself, and asserted twice that new-prefix creation was absent from the live path. That was wrong.

And the mechanism was demonstrated this morning, by our own test. The 1global push on prefix 192 sent Action A with a blank id, and Sippy responded by allocating a brand new rate id, 9221, for the new effective interval. A blank id producing a fresh id is the same allocation that a genuinely new prefix needs.

What that means for Aura. The five missing Business Class prefixes would most likely be created rather than dropped. Not certain, because 192 already existed as a prefix and Sippy was creating a new dated row for it, whereas Aura needs a prefix that does not exist at all. Those are adjacent but not identical.
