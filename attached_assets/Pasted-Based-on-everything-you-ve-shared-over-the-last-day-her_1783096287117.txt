One Improvement I'd Recommend

Instead of truncating logs every 6 hours, use logrotate for all Asterisk logs. This preserves history while keeping disk usage under control.

For example:

/var/log/asterisk/*.log
/var/log/asterisk/full
{
    daily
    rotate 14
    compress
    missingok
    notifempty
    copytruncate
}

That way you retain troubleshooting information without filling the disk.
