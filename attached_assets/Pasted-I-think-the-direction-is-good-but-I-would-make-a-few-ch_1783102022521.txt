Production Safety

Since your platform is production-first, I would deploy this in phases:

Sprint 1
XML-RPC telemetry
No interval changes
Observe for 24–48 hours
Sprint 2
Increase intervals
Compare calls/hour before and after
Sprint 3
Convert heavy schedulers to event-driven/on-demand
Keep telemetry to verify the reduction

This phased approach lets you measure the impact and provides evidence back to the Sippy team that the load has been reduced, while minimizing the risk of accidentally affecting live monitoring.
