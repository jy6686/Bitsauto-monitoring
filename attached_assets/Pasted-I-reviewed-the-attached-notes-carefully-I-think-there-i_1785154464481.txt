Not:

Option 2

My recommendation for BitsAuto

I would not spend time polishing the manual workflow yet.

I would spend one focused investigation answering a single question:

If the answer is:

internal PHP function

Since your stated product goal is zero manual work, I would not sign off on Option B as the final architecture yet.
